poly — polynomial arithmetic over a [field::Field] (Phase 0).

This is the second building block the rest of the pipeline sits on: everything from “AIR constraints” to “FRI folding” is, underneath, operations on polynomials over a finite field. This crate keeps those operations small and inspectable:

  • Polynomial — coefficient-form polynomials with schoolbook + - * and Horner evaluation.
  • fft — O(n log n) evaluation/interpolation on a power-of-two domain of roots of unity (the engine every other module here uses).
  • interpolate — turning (x, y) points into a polynomial, either generically (Lagrange, any domain) or fast (FFT, roots-of-unity domain).
  • lde — low-degree extension: evaluating a degree-<n polynomial on a coset blowup times larger than its “natural” domain, the step that turns an execution trace into something checkable via FRI.

Polynomial
A polynomial in coefficient form: coeffs[i] is the coefficient of x^i. Stored low-degree-first, with no trailing zero coefficients (Polynomial::new trims them), so two polynomials that are mathematically equal always have coeffs vectors that are equal too.
