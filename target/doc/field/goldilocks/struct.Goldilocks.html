impl Field for Goldilocks

Source§

fn inverse(&self) -> Option<Self>

Fermat’s little theorem: a^(p-2) == a^-1 for a != 0, since GF(p)* has order p - 1. Simpler and safer to get right than extended-Euclid at this word size; performance can be revisited later without changing the trait’s contract.

Source§

const TWO_ADICITY: u32 = 32

log2 of the largest power-of-two subgroup of the multiplicative group, i.e. the largest k such that 2^k | (p - 1). FFTs of size up to 2^TWO_ADICITY are supported.
